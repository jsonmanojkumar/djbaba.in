About DJ Baba

  • DJ Baba is a professional DJ and event management service based in Saharanpur, known for energetic music and well-organized events.
  • We specialize in weddings, parties, and corporate functions with high-quality sound systems and creative lighting setups.

  • DJ Sound & Music Setup – Powerful DJ sound, live mixing, and full party vibes for every celebration.
  • Lighting & Decoration – Stage lighting, home and venue decoration with theme-based designs.
  • Catering Booking – Hygienic, delicious food services with customizable menus for all events.

  • Our focus is on quality service, timely setup, and complete customer satisfaction to make your event unforgettable.

Our goal is simple – make your event legendary.

Our Services

DJ Sound

We provide complete DJ and lighting solutions for weddings and events. High-energy DJ music with professional sound system. Advanced laser lights, LED dance floor, and special effects available. Custom lighting designed as per your stage and event theme. Perfect setup to create a powerful and unforgettable party atmosphere.

Lighting Decoration

We provide beautiful home decoration services for weddings and functions. Ganglow (tent) decoration with attractive designs and quality materials. Catering Booking

We provide professional catering booking services for all types of events. Wide range of food options including vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus. Fresh, hygienic food prepared by experienced chefs. Menu customization available as per your event and budget. Quality service and delicious taste to satisfy all your guests.
